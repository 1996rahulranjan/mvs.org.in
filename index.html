About Us

Mulnivasi Vidyarthi Sangh (MVS) is a national student organization dedicated to organizing, educating, and empowering students belonging to the Mulnivasi Bahujan communities, including Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), and minority communities. Inspired by the revolutionary ideas of Mahatma Jyotiba Phule, Savitribai Phule, and Bharat Ratna Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, MVS works to promote social awareness, educational advancement, leadership development, and constitutional values among students. The organization believes that students are the future leaders of society and must play an active role in building a nation based on Justice, Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity. Through seminars, study circles, leadership training, social awareness campaigns, cultural activities, and democratic movements, MVS seeks to develop socially conscious and responsible youth leadership.

Our Mission

Educate, Organize & Agitate for Social Justice and Empowerment

🎓 Student Empowerment

Organizing Mulnivasi Bahujan students (SC, ST, OBC and Minority communities) across India for their welfare, leadership development, and all-round growth.

🏛 Educational Development

Promoting quality education, establishing study circles, libraries, educational institutions, research centers, and creating opportunities for meaningful learning and skill development.

⚖ Constitutional Rights & Social Justice

Protecting the fundamental rights guaranteed by the Constitution of India and supporting students facing discrimination, injustice, or social challenges through democratic means.

🌏 Leadership & Social Change

Developing student leadership based on the vision of Babasaheb Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and engaging youth in social awareness, community service, democratic participation, and nation-building.
