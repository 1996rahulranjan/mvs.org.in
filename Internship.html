MVS Internship Program

Mulnivasi Vidyarthi Sangh (MVS) proudly launches its Internship Program to support students from SC, ST and OBC communities by providing practical experience, leadership opportunities and skill development.

Who Can Apply?

🎓 Eligibility

Students belonging to SC, ST and OBC categories from schools, colleges and universities can apply.

🚀 Career Growth

Gain practical experience, leadership skills, teamwork exposure and networking opportunities.

🌍 National Platform

Become a part of a nationwide student organization and contribute to impactful initiatives.

Internship Benefits

📚 Skill Development

Communication, management and professional skills.

🤝 Team Collaboration

Work with dedicated MVS members.

🏆 Leadership Experience

Learn event management and leadership.

🌐 Networking

Build valuable connections.
